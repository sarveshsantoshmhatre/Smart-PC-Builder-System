CLOUD AI

The assistant is cloud-backed by the project's Puter Worker. Your browser sends the question and current build context to the worker; no AI model is downloaded to your device.

The selected open/free model and Puter authentication token are kept inside the serverless Worker, not in the public website. Puter free-model availability and provider limits can change over time.
